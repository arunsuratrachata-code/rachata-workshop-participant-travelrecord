        About the distances
        
        
        Road distance is the shortest driving route from OpenStreetMap (OSRM). Straight line is the distance as the crow flies. Home locations come from the address on the card: usually the subdistrict (ตำบล) centre unless the village is found, so check the pin when an exact distance matters.
